In this session, we'll critically examine the limitations of large language models (LLMs) in the context of business decision-making. Learn why relying on LLMs for critical tasks can lead to automation bias and unforeseen consequences, as they lack true understanding despite their impressive outputs. We'll discuss the core distinction between recreating facts and possessing genuine knowledge, and why this matters for reliable business processes. By applying a Toyota-inspired approach, we'll explore how to simplify processes before automation, ensuring that any use of LLMs remains focused on language tasks rather than decision-making.

This talk is perfect for anyone interested in AI, from leaders to engineers. Join the conversation with a fresh perspective on how to leverage AI responsibly and effectively, ensuring that human insight remains at the forefront of decision-making.
